# Noor Solar Energy — Bangla Localization Glossary

This glossary defines standard terminology, technical preservation rules, and stylistic standards used across Noor Solar Energy's Bengali content.

---

## 1. Brand & Voice Principles

- **Tone & Style**: Friendly, warm, natural spoken-standard Bangla (চলিত ভাষা). Conversational B2B register suitable for Bangladeshi business owners, engineers, and EPC contractors.
- **Address Form**: Always use **"আপনি"** (polite, respectful, relaxed). Never use "তুমি" or "তুই".
- **Brand Name**:
  - Latin Logo: Keep `"Noor Solar Energy"` in logo and brand visuals.
  - Running Bangla Text: `"নূর সোলার এনার্জি"` (or `"নূর সোলার"` where space is compact).
- **Punctuation**: Use Bengali Dari (`।`) for full stops. Keep standard commas, colons, hyphens, and question marks (`?`).
- **Numbers**: Keep Western Arabic numerals (`0-9`) as established in Task I18N-A for technical clarity (e.g. `620W`, `48V`, `10,000+`).

## 3. Preserved Latin Technical Terms (Allowlist)

These terms must remain in Latin script exactly as written, without phonetic transliteration:

- **Cell & Panel Technologies**: `TOPCon`, `PERC`, `Mono PERC`, `N-Type`, `P-Type`, `Bifacial`, `Half-Cut`, `Monocrystalline`, `Polycrystalline`
- **Battery Chemistries**: `LiFePO4`, `Lithium Iron Phosphate`, `BMS`, `Rack-mount`, `Wall-mount`
- **Electrical & Engineering**: `MPPT`, `PWM`, `Pure Sine Wave`, `Hybrid`, `On-Grid`, `Off-Grid`, `Grid-Tie`, `IP65`, `IP67`, `IP20`
- **Standards & Certifications**: `IEC`, `ISO`, `CE`, `TUV`, `UL`, `Tier 1`, `RoHS`
- **Units & Measures**: `W`, `kW`, `MW`, `V`, `A`, `Ah`, `kWh`, `MWh`, `Hz`, `kg`, `mm`, `°C`
- **Product Codes & Model Numbers**: e.g. `NS-M620T`, `NS-L48100`, `NS-H50K`, `RS485`, `CAN`
