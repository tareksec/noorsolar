# PRD — Noor Solar Energy website

Status: v1 (draft for build). Owner: site owner. Lead developer: Claude. Builder: local AI agent.

## 1. Purpose

A fast, impressive website that presents three product lines and turns visitors into **quote requests**.

- Business: Noor Solar Energy imports and sells **solar panels, lithium-ion batteries, inverters** in Bangladesh. Sales are mainly **B2B / bulk**, with some consumer sales.
- The site sells nothing online. It **shows products** and **collects quote requests** (also via WhatsApp and phone).
- The owner manages all content from an **admin panel**. No developer needed for daily edits.

## 2. Users

| User | Wants | Main action |
|---|---|---|
| Business buyer (factory, EPC company, dealer, project manager) | Compare specs, check bulk availability, get a price for a quantity | Request a quote for one or more products |
| Home or shop owner | Understand which product fits, talk to a person | Call, WhatsApp, or request a quote |
| Site owner / staff | Update products and see new requests | Admin panel |

## 3. Goals and success measures

1. A visitor reaches a product and sends a quote request in **under 60 seconds**.
2. The design feels premium and modern, with smooth animation, **without hurting speed**: mobile Lighthouse targets Performance ≥ 90, Accessibility ≥ 95, SEO ≥ 95 on the home page.
3. The owner can add a product with images and specs **without help**.
4. No quote request is lost: every request is stored in the database and visible in the admin.

## 4. Scope

### In scope (v1)

- Public site: Home, Products, Category, Product detail, Blog (/blog and /blog/[slug]), About, Contact, 404
- Product Reviews: public submission (when enabled), moderation, display on product pages
- Quote request form (general and per product), stored in the database
- WhatsApp, call and email buttons everywhere they help
- Admin panel: login, dashboard, categories, products (images, specs, datasheet upload, SEO), blog management (Markdown editor, live preview, cover image, status), product reviews moderation, quote inbox, site settings, and trust content (statistics, certifications, partners, testimonials, FAQ)
- Trust content sections on the public site, shipped with clearly flagged SAMPLE content that the owner replaces (see section 7)
- Demo data for all categories and products (generated by the agent) and 3 educational sample blog posts
- SEO basics, sitemap, robots, structured data
- Deploy to Hostinger

### Out of scope (v1)

- Cart, checkout, online payment, customer accounts, order tracking
- ERP integration (data layer must allow it later)
- Multi-language (structure should not block adding Bangla later)
- Solar calculator, system builder, installation booking

## 5. Public site requirements

### 5.1 Global

- Header: glass navigation bar per `DESIGN.md`: logo circle, menu, tab links (Solar Panels, Batteries, Inverters), and a "Request quote" pill button. Full mobile menu.
- Floating WhatsApp button.
- Footer: company name, one-line description, quick links, contact block, address. All values from site settings.
- Every page has a clear route to "Request quote" within one click.

### 5.2 Home page (top to bottom)

1. **Hero:** headline, one sentence, two buttons ("Request quote", "Browse products"), a row of three neutral feature items, and an interactive glass illustration of panel, battery and inverter. Draft copy (owner will edit): *"Solar panels, lithium batteries and inverters, supplied in bulk."*
2. **Category dock:** three pill cards (Solar Panels, Lithium-ion Batteries, Inverters), each with a round thumbnail, one spec line and a pill button.
3. **Business statistics band:** animated counters (for example years in business, projects supplied, happy clients, success rate). Editable in the admin. Ships with SAMPLE values.
4. **Scroll-linked category story:** one panel per category with its visual and key specs. Includes counters computed from real product data (highest panel wattage, largest battery capacity, largest inverter rating).
5. **Featured products:** horizontal carousel of products marked "featured" in the admin, with arrow buttons and drag.
6. **Certifications:** cards or badges with name, issuer and image. Editable. SAMPLE content until replaced.
7. **How ordering works:** a real sequence: request a quote, confirm specifications and quantity, receive a quotation, confirm and arrange delivery.
8. **Partners and clients:** logo marquee. Editable. SAMPLE placeholders until replaced.
9. **Testimonials:** slider with quote, name, role and company. Editable. SAMPLE content until replaced.
10. **FAQ:** accordion, editable in the admin.
11. **Closing call to action with a compact quote form**, then footer.

Sections with no visible items do not render.

Marquee band, counters and pinned sections are allowed where they help. Do not overload the page.

### 5.3 Products page

- Category tabs and a search box, grid of product cards.
- Card: image, name, category, one or two key specs, stock status, "View details" and "Request quote".
- Price is shown **only** when the product has "show price" enabled. Otherwise show "Request quote".
- Empty state: says what to do next.

### 5.4 Category page

- Category intro, then the same grid filtered to that category.

### 5.5 Product detail page

- Image gallery with zoom or swipe.
- Name, short description, category, brand (optional), model, stock status, MOQ, lead time (all optional).
- Specification table (label and value rows, editable per product).
- Datasheet download link when a file or URL exists.
- "Request quote" opens a form with the product prefilled (quantity, company, phone).
- Related products from the same category.

### 5.6 Quote request form

Fields: name (required), phone (required), company, email, product (prefilled if any), quantity, delivery location, message.
Behavior: inline validation, clear success and error states, spam protection (honeypot and rate limit), no page reload. Saved to the database. Success state offers WhatsApp as a follow-up.

### 5.7 About and Contact

- About: short company text (from settings), what we supply, how we work. No invented history or numbers.
- Contact: form, phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map placeholder, opening hours (all from settings).

## 6. Admin panel requirements

Route: `/admin`. Simple, fast, clean. Uses shadcn/ui styling. Decorative animation is not needed here.

| Area | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Login | Email and password, secure session, logout, change password |
| Dashboard | Counts of products, categories and new quote requests; latest 5 requests |
| Categories | Create, edit, reorder, activate or hide. Fields: name, slug, description, image |
| Products | Create, edit, delete, duplicate. Fields: name, slug, category, short description, description, brand, model, images (multiple, reorder, alt text), specifications (add, remove, reorder rows), stock status, MOQ, lead time, price and "show price" toggle, datasheet upload/link, SEO fields (metaTitle, metaDescription), featured, active |
| Blog | Create, edit, delete, draft/publish. Fields: title, slug, excerpt, content (Markdown with live preview), cover image & alt, tags, author, SEO fields (metaTitle, metaDescription) |
| Reviews | List, filter by status and product, add admin review, approve, reject, delete, bulk approve. Toggle public submission in settings |
| Quote inbox | List with filters (new, contacted, closed), detail view, change status, add internal note, phone and WhatsApp shortcut, export CSV |
| Settings | Company name, tagline, phone, WhatsApp, email, address, opening hours, social links, hero text, reviews.publicEnabled toggle |
| Trust content | Statistics, certifications, partners, testimonials, FAQ: create, edit, delete, reorder, show or hide, upload logos and photos. SAMPLE badge on unreviewed items; dashboard warning with the count of sample items still live |

Validation and clear error messages on every form. Confirm before delete.

## 7. Demo data specification

The site launches with demo data so every screen looks real before the owner adds real products. The agent generates it in the seed script (`TRD.md` section 3). The owner replaces or deletes it from the admin.

**Categories (3):** Solar Panels, Lithium-ion Batteries, Inverters. Each has a name, slug, one-sentence description and an image.

**Products (15, five per category).** Names are generic spec-style names. Do not use real brand names.

| Category | Demo products |
|---|---|
| Solar Panels | N-Type TOPCon Bifacial Module 620W; N-Type TOPCon Bifacial Module 585W; Mono PERC Half-Cut Module 550W; Mono PERC Half-Cut Module 450W; Mono PERC Module 400W |
| Lithium-ion Batteries | 48V 100Ah LiFePO4 Rack Battery; 48V 200Ah LiFePO4 Rack Battery; 51.2V 280Ah LiFePO4 Rack Battery; 12.8V 200Ah LiFePO4 Battery; 48V 100Ah Wall-Mount LiFePO4 Battery |
| Inverters | 3kW Hybrid Inverter (single phase); 5kW Hybrid Inverter (single phase); 8kW Hybrid Inverter (single phase); 10kW Hybrid Inverter (three phase); 30kW On-Grid String Inverter (three phase) |

**Per product:**

- 3 images (front, angled, detail) with real alt text
- 8 to 12 specification rows that are realistic and consistent with the name (for example a 620W panel gets sensible voltage, current, efficiency, dimensions and weight; a 48V 100Ah battery is about 5.12 kWh)
- A short description (one sentence) and a longer description (two short paragraphs)
- A mix of stock statuses (In stock, Incoming, On request)
- MOQ and lead time filled in for some products and empty for others, to test empty states
- Three or four products marked featured, spread across categories
- All marked `isDemo`; price hidden

**Site settings demo values:** company name "Noor Solar Energy", the phone and WhatsApp numbers from the current site, placeholder email and address, and 6 demo FAQ items.

**Rules:**

- **Sample content policy.** The owner wants certifications, statistics (counters, success rate), partners and testimonials visible now and will replace the values himself before or after launch.
  - Every seeded row of these types carries `isSample = true`.
  - Sample values look plausible but generic. No real company names, real people, real logos or named standards (use names like "Quality management certificate", not "ISO 9001").
  - The admin shows a SAMPLE badge on those rows and a dashboard warning counting them. Editing and saving a row, or pressing "Mark as real", clears the flag.
  - Environment variable `HIDE_SAMPLE_CONTENT=true` hides all sample rows on the public site. Sections left with no visible items do not render.
  - Sample content must be reviewed and replaced before the public launch; this is on the launch checklist.
- Product names use no real brand names or logos.
- Price is off by default.
- Images are procedural placeholders in the site's design style. If the owner adds real photos to `public/demo/photos/`, use those instead. See `AGENT.md` section 6.

## 8. Non-functional requirements

- **Performance:** fast on mid-range phones over average Bangladeshi mobile networks. Lazy load heavy visuals. Optimized images.
- **Responsive:** 360px to 1920px.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA target, keyboard usable, reduced-motion respected.
- **SEO:** unique titles and descriptions, sitemap, robots, structured data, clean URLs.
- **Security:** see `TRD.md` section 9.
- **Maintainability:** clear folders, typed data, one place for site settings.

## 9. Assumptions and open items

- ERP integration is not needed now. The owner may want it later.
- The domain `noorsolaren.com` currently points to an ERP storefront. Switching DNS to the new site ends that storefront. **Owner decides when.**
- Hosting: Hostinger plan with Node.js support. Whether Noor Solar shares the existing plan or gets its own is **to be confirmed** by the owner.
- Logo file and final contact details will be supplied later. Use placeholders until then.
- Whether the owner has verified certifications to show is **unknown**. Show none for now.
